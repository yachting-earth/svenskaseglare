2022-04-24 - Preveza

![](flyg.jpg)

Due to the long flight (3 hours or so) and being jet lagged (+1h..) we decided to stay and enjoy Preveza for a day. For you who haven't been there, Preveza is a small town that became part of Greece 1912. Preveza that was built 11th century on the remains of Vereniki that was founded by the Greek king of Epirus about 2300 years ago. The towns narrow streets are filled to the brim with small restaurants, coffeeshops and small stores and of course if there's a nice city quay and a small marina to stroll along.

Plötsligt hände det!! 632 dagar efter att vi lämnade Sverige bestämde sig min far för att det var dags att svänga förbi och hälsa på. Han gjorde det glasklart att det bara var för att han var nyfiken på vår nya båt och att han saknade segling lite, det hade INGENTING att göra med segling i Medelhavet eller Grekland att göra då de små öarna här bara är en bit land i vattnet, och han har han minsann hemma med. För att göra det hela riktigt klart och tydligt så är maten i Grekland är inte ens i närheten av de fantastiska rätterna som han lagar hemma. Mer om allt det senare...

Den initiala planen var att tillbringa lite tid Ionian och sedan ge oss över till östkusten och Aten och hitta ett flyg tillbaks norrut. Men som det alltid är med planer och båtar, ju bättre du planerar desto bättre vet du hur det inte kommer att bli. Det slutade med tre trevliga veckor i Ionian, där vi hittade ett gäng pärlor både via segel och bil.

På grund av den långa flygresan (ungefär 3 timmar) och all jetlag (+1h..) beslutade vi oss för att stanna och njuta av Preveza ett litet tag. För er som inte har varit där är Preveza en liten stad som blev en del av Grekland 1912. Preveza byggdes på 1100-talet på ruinerna av Vereniki, som grundades av den grekiska kungen av Epirus för cirka 2300 år sedan. Stadens smala gator är fyllda till brädden med små restauranger, kaféer och affärer, självklart finns det en trevlig stadskaj och en liten marina att promenera runt i.

*Vonitsa 2022-04-26*

Our second stop was just a few (8 to be precise) nautical miles away, for you landlubbers one nautical mile is 1852m, was Vonitsa that is a sweet little town located in the Amvrakikos gulf just inside of Preveza.

![](vonitsa-1.png)

![](Johnny-vid-rodret.jpg)

![](vonitsa-bay-evening.jpg)

We dropped our anchor in the calm east bay outside of Vonitsa next to our friends Magnus and Lucina on S/Y Sarita. Compared to Preveza Vonitsa is a small and calm village that is perfect to hang out in when you want to relax and have a coffee or two.

![](Vonitsa-Ankring.jpeg)

One of the major attractions of Vonitsa is the castle that oversees the bay and entire town, we planned to visit it last year but didn't get around to do so due to the heat. This year was different, we went ashore and took of by foot aiming for the big fort. By the time we got there (after a few wrong turns) the urge for both shade and food was way bigger than the old stones from back in the days so we turned around next to the gate and took aim at the first restaurant that served cold beer and had a nice view of the bay.

2022-04-29 - Nidri

After spending spending three nights at anchor outside of Vonitsa we decided that it was time to hoist our sails again and head south through the Lefkas canal down to Nidri on the east coast of Lefkas.

![](nidri.jpeg)

The 25 nm to Nidri allowed the Ionian weather to show its best side and we had a great day at sea with a lot of nice sailing in 10 to 25 knots of wind till we had to fire up the steel genua and motor the last few miles when the wind died on us. Could have been worse.

Nidri has in some strange way become our home away from home in the Ionian, a super touristic place that boils with both turists and restaurants but it's still a nice place to hang out and enjoy drinks at Cafe di Paris in the evening and grab a gyros or two at Stathis with a cold beer.

2022-05-1 - Midsommarviken

Midsommarviken is Swedish for "Midsummer bay", a small bay on Meganisi with crystal clear water. The bay itself got nothing special to offer beside clear water and really crappy cell connection. A really nice and calm stopover.
![](midsommarviken.jpeg)
![](midsommarviken.png)

Since the weather wasn't the best during our stay we decided to move on to Vathi a bit south instead.

2022-05-02 - Vathi
![](vathi.jpeg)

We spent a few days at the windy anchorage in Vathi and had the pleasure to meet up with the lovely couple Ingela and Lars on Baravara that we also spent the winter next to in Messolongi. That combined with discounted 5 liters boxes of wine made or stay quite interesting. We didn't do a lot of sightseeing in Vathi, honestly there isn't a lot to see except for the lovely view from the boat, high mountains and colourful houses around the small bay makes the trip there totally worth it (even without the discounted wine).
